Questions

The things you're already thinking.

I'm full after half a plate. How does this help?
The meals are built dense, so a plate you can finish carries more calories and protein than one you can't. It doesn't make you hungrier. It makes every meal you do eat count for more, and shakes cover the gaps.
I don't want to end up skinny-fat.
Your route has a body-fat cap. When you reach it the app switches you to a short trim phase, then back to building. You're never on an open-ended bulk.
I can't afford to eat 3,500 calories a day.
One shop a week off a single list, batch-cooked, with ingredients the meals share. Add your own prices for a running total.
I don't have time to cook.
34 of the 81 meals take 15 minutes or less. Most of the week's food comes off one prep session.
Do I need ChatGPT or another AI?
No. There's no AI in the app. Your route, your program and your meals are built from published rules, on your phone, in about a minute.
How much does JSON.fit cost?
JSON.fit is free: meal plans, training programs, shopping lists and meal prep, with no ads and no account.
