What to eat, every day

Swap anything.
Your macros hold.

Every day shows what to eat and when. Not feeling Tuesday's lunch? Swap it for another meal you like. The app re-balances that day's portions so it still lands on your calories and protein, and tells you what it changed.

  • Only meals that keep the day on target are offered
  • Portions shift in small steps, never a different meal plan
  • Your shopping list updates with it

FAQ

Common questions.

I already train. My food is the mess.
Start with the meals. Pick the ones you like and the app plans the week around your calories and protein, writes the shopping list and plans one prep session. Each day shows what to eat and when.
I don't have time to cook.
Most of the week's food comes off one prep session. 34 of the 81 meals take 15 minutes or less, and the app picks what goes in the fridge and what goes in the freezer.
Won't I get sick of eating the same thing?
One cook covers different meals across the week, not one dish seven times. Swap any meal for another you like and the app re-balances the day so it still lands on your calories and protein.
Is it only for building muscle?
No. It's for men and women who want to lose weight, build muscle or both. Your route sets a goal weight and a date, with the phases in between.
Do I need ChatGPT or another AI?
No. There's no AI in the app. Your route, your program and your meals are built from published rules, on your phone, in about a minute.
How much does JSON.fit cost?
JSON.fit is free: meal plans, training programs, shopping lists and meal prep, with no ads and no account.
