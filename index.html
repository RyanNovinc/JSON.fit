FAQ

Common questions.

Every meal here is built dense, so the plate you can finish carries the calories, and most of the week's food comes off one shop and one prep session. You stop having to cook your way to 3,000 calories every night.
Because I built it for the same problem. I can't gain weight, I work full time, and I'd rather cook once a week than every night. Every meal and every number in the app is what I use on my own bulk, and the scans are public in The Lab. If it's working on me, you'll see it there before you download anything.
Importing a plan takes a few minutes. After that it's one shop and one prep session for most of your meals, then quick logging as you go.
Because the AI doesn't set the rules. The prompt carries the set ranges per muscle, the rep bands, the rest and the reps in reserve from the studies on the research page, and a second pass checks the file against them before you import it. The AI only fills in the exercises for your equipment and your week.
It isn't for powerlifters chasing a total, or for anyone who wants a coach to talk to. It's for lifters who want a program built on the evidence, laid out for a year, and tracked without thinking about it. I run my own training from it, and the scans are in The Lab.
Every exercise comes with alternatives that hit the same muscle. Swap it mid-session and the sets, reps and rest carry over.
There isn't one. Most fitness apps charge $10–20 a month because they run AI on their own servers. JSON.fit uses the AI you already have, so there's no server cost to pass on. No ads, nothing sold. Your data stays on your device; the app only collects anonymous usage analytics to improve it.
The free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini all work. If you already pay for one, even better. JSON.fit itself has no AI costs to pass on.
Yes. Your workouts, meals and health data stay on your device and are never uploaded. There are no accounts. Like most apps, it uses anonymous, non-identifying analytics to see how it's used. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
