How it works

Built in seconds.
Not by a chatbot.

Nothing to set up, nothing to copy anywhere. The app does the planning itself, with the same rules a good coach would use, and shows you the work while it builds.

  1. 01

    Answer a few questions.

    Your body, your goal, how many days you can train, the kit you've got, and the meals you actually like. A couple of minutes, once.

  2. 02

    JSON.fit builds it.

    A week of meals that lands on your calories and protein every day, and a training program with the right sets for every muscle, deloads planned in.

  3. 03

    Cook and train.

    Every meal written step by step. Every set logged, with what you lifted last time and how hard to push today.

Macros built around your body

Swap anything.
Your macros hold.

Not feeling Tuesday's lunch? Swap it for another meal you like. The app re-balances that day's portions so it still lands on your calories and protein, and tells you what it changed.

  • Only meals that keep the day on target are offered
  • Portions shift in small steps, never a different meal plan
  • Your shopping list updates with it

Got food to use up?

Start with
what's in the fridge.

Tick the chicken, rice or eggs you already have. Meals that use them come first, or let the app fill your week from them. Anything you won't eat, or can't, stays out of the way.

  • Every food our meals use, by aisle, one tap each
  • Allergens flagged before you pick, foods you skip moved down
  • Your past picks a tap away: "Same as last week"

Meal prep

A week's cooking,
done in one go.

One shop, one prep session. The app plans which meals to batch-cook, what goes in the fridge, what goes in the freezer, and reminds you the night before to move tomorrow's food out to thaw.

  • Cook mode with every step and timer
  • Fridge and freezer planned for every portion
  • A reminder the night before anything needs thawing

Shopping

One list.
Priced at your store.

Every ingredient for the week on one list, grouped by aisle. Want to know what it'll cost? Send the list to your own AI and it comes back with the real products and prices at your store.

  • Tick things off as they go in the trolley
  • Leftovers you already have are marked
  • Prices are optional, and so is the AI

FAQ

Common questions.

How does JSON.fit work?
Answer a few questions about your body, your goal, the days you can train, the kit you have and the meals you like. The app builds your week of meals and your training program in seconds, right on your phone.
Do I need ChatGPT or another AI?
No. Everything is built in the app. If you like your own AI, you can still use it to customise a plan or to price your shopping list at your store, but both are optional.
How much does JSON.fit cost?
JSON.fit is free: meal plans, training programs, shopping lists and meal prep, with no ads and no account.
I'm full after half a plate. Does this help?
The meals are built dense, so a plate you can finish carries more calories and protein than a plate you can't. It doesn't make you hungrier. It makes every meal you do eat count for more.
I tried Sunday prep and quit. How is this different?
Every meal defaults to the fast version: jar sauce, minimal steps, real times. Most of the week's food comes off one shop and a short prep session, not an afternoon, and the app tells you what goes in the fridge and what goes in the freezer.
Does this work if I've got a full-time job?
That's exactly who it's built for. You answer a few questions once, the app builds the plan, then you cook in batches off a single shopping list. Eating enough fits around work instead of taking it over.
Can I change the plan once it's built?
Yes. Swap any meal for another you like and the app re-balances that day so it still hits your calories and protein. You can also change your picks and rebuild the week in seconds.
Can I train at home?
Yes. Programs are built for a full gym, a home gym, dumbbells and bands, or bodyweight only, from 1 to 6 days a week.
Is my data private with JSON.fit?
Yes. Your workouts, meals and health data stay on your device and are never uploaded. JSON.fit has no accounts and doesn't collect personal information. Like most apps and websites, it uses anonymous, non-identifying usage analytics (such as Google Analytics) to see how it's used and improve it. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
