What the app decides

Five numbers, set from the research.

Every program is built from these. The studies and confidence labels are on How we know.

Sets
Weekly hard sets per muscle, set to your levelPelland et al. 2026 · Schoenfeld et al. 2017 · Good
2×
Each muscle about twice a weekSchoenfeld et al. 2016, 2019 · Good
5–15
Most sets in the 5 to 15 rep rangeSchoenfeld et al. 2017 · Good
RIR

Rest
2 to 3 minutes on the big liftsSinger et al. 2024 · Schoenfeld et al. 2016 · Good
Deload
